Commonly Advertised Bonuses

WinPKR-related websites advertise welcome bonuses, first-deposit offers, daily bonuses, cashback, referral rewards and VIP promotions.

Read the Conditions

A bonus is not necessarily free cash. Check the minimum deposit, wagering requirement, eligible games, maximum bonus, expiry, maximum eligible winnings and withdrawal conditions.

For example, a 20× wagering requirement on a Rs1,000 bonus would mean Rs20,000 of qualifying wagers before the relevant condition is satisfied.

VIP & Loyalty Programs

Some pages promote loyalty points, cashback and higher limits. Playing more to reach a VIP level can also mean risking more money, so never use a loyalty target as a reason to chase losses.
