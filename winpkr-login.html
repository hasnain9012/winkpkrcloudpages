Account Security

  • Use a unique password.
  • Never share your password.
  • Never share OTP codes.
  • Avoid public computers for financial accounts.
  • Check the domain before entering credentials.
  • Use the platform's official recovery process if access is lost.

Registration

Registration requirements can change. A real-money service may require identity verification before financial activity. Avoid creating multiple accounts simply to collect promotions.

Login Problems

Check your username or phone number, password, internet connection, account status and the exact website address. Never send credentials to someone claiming to be support.
