APK Safety Checklist

  • Verify the source domain.
  • Identify the developer.
  • Check the APK signature and package name.
  • Check a SHA-256 hash if available.
  • Scan the APK.
  • Review requested permissions.
  • Read the privacy policy and terms.
  • Avoid modified or “unlimited money” APKs.

Payment Safety

Check the current payment-provider terms before depositing. A payment logo shown on a third-party website is not proof of provider approval.

Legal Questions

Pakistan has gambling-related legislation, including the Prevention of Gambling Act, 1977. Whether a particular online service is lawful can depend on applicable federal or provincial law, the activity and licensing circumstances. Seek current legal advice for a definitive answer.

Pakistan Code — Prevention of Gambling Act, 1977

Responsible Gaming

Do not treat gambling as a job or salary. Set a budget, do not chase losses, never borrow to gamble and never use money needed for rent, food, bills or emergencies.
